## 2. Core Drafting & Subject Rules

### B. Dynamic Research Topic (`{Research Topic}`)
- Generated by LLM (Gemini 3.6 Flash) as a concise, title-cased 3–6 word phrase (e.g., `Reliable AI-Driven Software Engineering`, `Explainable AI in Healthcare`, `Trustworthy Deep Learning Systems`).
- Captures the exact technical bridge between Forhad's background (ML, XAI, AI-driven SE) and the professor's verified 2025/2026 publications.
- Fallback: Uses cleaned primary interest from the sheet's `Research Area` column.

### C. Intake Semester
- Standardized across all 10 countries: **`Spring/Fall 2027`**.

### D. Attachments & Profile Anchoring
- Explicitly states: *"I have attached my resume and academic transcript for your review."*
- Word count strictly between 115 and 145 words (maximum ceiling: 150 words).

## 4. Concurrency, Locking & Same-Department Rules

1. **Department Locking**:
   - `Contact Group (Lock)` identifies the department/institution block (e.g., `DE-TUM-INFORMATICS`, `US-CMU-CS`).
   - If a professor in a contact group is currently `Needs Review`, `Approved`, or `Applied`, that group is locked to prevent sending competing emails to the same department.
2. **Same-Department 9-Day Rule**:
   - For rows marked `Applied`, if more than 9 days have passed since the first contact date, the lock for that department can be released to allow contacting another professor in that department.
3. **Skipping Locked Groups**:
   - `draft_next` automatically skips locked groups and selects the highest-priority unlocked candidate in the filtered country.

---

## 5. Technical Safeguards & Error Handling

1. **Scraping**:
   - Sorts Google Scholar queries by `&sortby=pubdate` to guarantee recency (2025/2026).
   - If Google Scholar returns 429, captcha, or empty results, the system automatically falls back to the Semantic Scholar Graph API.
2. **Rate Limiting**:
   - A polite 1.5–2.5 second sleep is inserted between sheet row updates.
   - LLM calls use Gemini 3.6 Flash with automatic retry on transient quota errors.
3. **Quality Gate**:
   - Auto-shortener reduces drafts exceeding 150 words down to ~135–145 words.
   - Updates sheet with:
     - `Pipeline Status` ➔ `"Needs Review"`
     - `Email Subject` ➔ `Exploring {Degree} Research on {Topic} – Forhad Uddin Ahmed`
     - `Email Draft` ➔ Formatted email body under 150 words
     - `LLM Research Summary` ➔ 2–4 sentence technical analysis
     - `Fit Score` ➔ 1–10 score
